# Scientific references

1. A. S. Tijsseling, Q. Hou, Z. Bozkus and J. Laanearu (2016). *Improved One-Dimensional Models for Rapid Emptying and Filling of Pipelines*. Journal of Pressure Vessel Technology **138**, 031301. https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4031508  
   **Why I used it:** This paper helped me understand the moving-column mass and momentum balances (especially Eq. 9), stationary holdup and a simplified horizontal-pipe case. The public example does not reproduce the full published model.

2. X. Chen, Q. Hou, J. Laanearu and A. S. Tijsseling (2024). *Experimental data on filling and emptying of a large-scale pipeline*. Scientific Data **11**, 603. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03441-7  
   **Why it is useful:** It provides public experimental context for pipeline filling and emptying. I have not copied its measurements, figures or apparatus details into the examples.

The illustrative ideal-gas/polytropic relation and volume-balance identity are standard modelling relationships; they are not themselves experimental findings or evidence that the full industrial model has been validated.

3. J. Laanearu et al. (2012). *Emptying of Large-Scale Pipeline by Pressurized Air*. Journal of Hydraulic Engineering **138**, 1090–1100. https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0000631  
   **Why I included it:** It provides context for air-driven discharge and liquid left behind during emptying.

4. J. Laanearu et al. (2014). *Semi-Empirical Method for Estimation of Energy Losses in a Large-Scale Pipeline*. Procedia Engineering **70**, 969–977. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.proeng.2014.02.108  
   **Why I included it:** It discusses energy-loss modelling for large-scale pipelines.

5. A. Cioncolini and J. R. Thome (2012). *Void Fraction Prediction in Annular Two-Phase Flow*. International Journal of Multiphase Flow **43**, 72–84. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmultiphaseflow.2012.03.003  
6. A. Cioncolini and J. R. Thome (2013). *Liquid Film Circumferential Asymmetry Prediction in Horizontal Annular Two-Phase Flow*. International Journal of Multiphase Flow **51**, 44–54. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmultiphaseflow.2012.12.003  
7. A. Cioncolini and J. R. Thome (2017). *Pressure Drop Prediction in Annular Two-Phase Flow in Macroscale Tubes and Channels*. International Journal of Multiphase Flow **89**, 321–330. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmultiphaseflow.2016.11.003  
   **Not implemented:** Items 5–7 are useful further reading on annular flow. Their correlations are not implemented or validated in this repository.

The equations in this repository were reconstructed into simplified public Python examples with AI assistance. These sources are not papers I authored, and the original industrial code and measurements remain confidential. See the [README](../README.md).
